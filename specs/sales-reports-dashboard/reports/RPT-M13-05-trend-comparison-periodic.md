## Calculations (CONFIRMED inline SQL)
**Sales comparison** (`SalesComparisoneReportDal.cs:15-19`): per district D: `SaleValue = SUM(I.DeliveryTpGrandTotal)` for `DeliveryInvoiceStatus IN ('Full','Partial')` and YEAR/MONTH(InvoiceDate)=month of @ReportDate; `PreviousMonthSaleValue` = same for the previous month; `DiffInAmount = PreviousMonthSaleValue - ISNULL(SaleValue,0)`; `DiffInPercent = ROUND(CONVERT(DECIMAL(18,2), ISNULL(SaleValue,0) / (PreviousMonthSaleValue/100)), 2)` -> current as % of previous (not growth %); NULL/zero previous month gives NULL or a divide-by-zero error. Districts are matched by `DistrictCode = I.DisCode`.
**Product-wise national** (`ProductWiseNationalSalesReportDal.cs`): per product, for the month of @FromDate (M) and M-1, M-2, M-3: `DeliveryTotal = SUM(DeliveryTotalQuantity)`, `SaleValue = SUM(DeliveryNetAmount)` over delivered lines (`Full`,`Partial`) by month of `InvoiceDate`; `ContributionPercent = ROUND(CONVERT(DECIMAL(18,2), CurrentMonthSaleValue / (national CurrentMonth SUM(DeliveryNetAmount) / 100)), 2)`; ordered by current sale value descending. Zone variant: same but per fixed RegionCode 'DK-100','CM-100','SL-100','BG-100','KL-100' (columns [DeliveryTotal-XX-100], [SaleValue-XX-100]) plus month totals.
