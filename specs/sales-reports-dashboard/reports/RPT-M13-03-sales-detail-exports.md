Viewer behaviour: `AllSalesreportViewer` and `FinalSalesViewer` contain two if/else blocks that both call `ExportToHttpResponse` in one request depending on `NationalReport` and `Session["SalesReport"]` - the second export after the first response has been ended is unreachable in practice (INFERRED). Class name `SInventory_RPTVIEW_AllSalesreportViewer` is also used inside MIOProductSalesreportViewer.aspx.cs (copy-paste). Rules: BR-M13-050, BR-M13-051.
